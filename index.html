Construction Phase

From design handover to site delivery, one continuous record.

JKR BINAXONE follows the project past tender. Site teams, PMC, and contractors work off the same model and documents that design signed off on, with construction-grade workflows layered on top.

Site Progress Tracking

Capture daily progress photos and geotagged inspections against the BIM model, then let AI write the site diary: reading the photos, updating percentage-complete, and flagging where the site is drifting from the programme.

On-Plan Pin Documents & Defect Management

Pinpoint defects directly on the model or drawing. AI classifies each defect from its photo, routes it to the right contractor, and drafts the closure report. Nothing slips between handover and DLP.

Built-in JKR SPB Quality Forms

Field teams always work from the latest JKR SPB site record templates, and AI pre-fills them from data already captured on site, then summarises quality trends across forms so project leads see problems while they're still cheap to fix.

Facility Management Phase

The model keeps working, long after handover.

Most as-built models end up in a folder nobody opens. In JKR BINAXONE, handover is where the model starts its second life: a live, BIM-based CMMS run on the same model your team built. No re-keying asset data, no starting from zero.

Operations · BIM-based CMMS

Live Asset Register on the Model

Every pump, chiller, and switchboard carries its specs, warranty, and O&M data on the model. AI builds the register for you, extracting it from handover documents automatically, and answers questions like "which assets are still under warranty?" in plain language.

Predictive Maintenance & Digital Twin

Connect IoT, SCADA, and BAS feeds to turn the model into a living digital twin. AI watches live telemetry and years of service history to predict failures, schedule the right maintenance at the right time, and raise work orders before breakdowns, not after.

Space & Lifecycle Management

Track occupancy, condition, and remaining life across the whole estate. AI forecasts renewal budgets and flags where intervention now extends asset life later. Longer life cycles, fewer surprises, lower total cost of ownership.

Frequently asked questions.

What is a CDE and why do I need one?
A Common Data Environment is the single, controlled source of truth for project information: drawings, models, correspondence, approvals. It replaces email chains and shared drives with structured workflows, audit trails, and enforced review gates. For any project of meaningful size, a CDE isn't optional; it's the only way coordination holds together.
Is JKR BINAXONE compliant with ISO 19650 and JKR BIM standards?
Yes. The platform is built around ISO 19650 information states and JKR BIM standard deliverables, including BPEP structure, naming conventions, and transmittal workflows. Reviews, approvals, and digital signatures are captured in a form that maps directly to JKR audit expectations.
Where is my data stored?
On TM One Cloud Alpha, hosted in Cyberjaya, Malaysia, a Tier III data centre operated by a Malaysian vendor. Your project data is stored, processed, and backed up entirely within Malaysian jurisdiction.
What file formats are supported?
JKR BINAXONE is IFC-native and supports common open BIM formats, alongside standard document formats (PDF, DWG, DOCX, XLSX, images). Open formats are first-class citizens, not a second-tier export path.
How does JKR BINAXONE compare to other CDE platforms?
JKR BINAXONE is purpose-built around JKR workflows, Malaysian procurement practice, and local data-sovereignty requirements. You get strong technical capability alongside local hosting, support, and accountability, with licensing structures that align naturally with government and industry procurement in Malaysia.
Can JKR BINAXONE be deployed on-premise?
Yes. Enterprise engagements support dedicated tenancy on TM One Cloud Alpha, hybrid deployments, or full on-premise installation inside an agency's own data centre for the most sensitive programmes.
What does "post-quantum cryptography" mean for my documents?
Today's digital signatures can become forgeable once quantum computing matures. Infrastructure projects outlive the cryptography that signed them. Post-quantum algorithms are resistant to attacks from future quantum machines, which means a document signed in JKR BINAXONE today stays legally and technically verifiable decades from now, aligned with Malaysian national cybersecurity direction.
What is the BINAXONE AI Copilot Suite, and is it part of the CDE?
The Copilot Suite is a set of AI plugins that install directly into your design tools. The Revit copilot is available now, with AutoCAD, Civil 3D, and MS Project coming soon. The AI builds, tags, and fills your model while you review and approve, and runs JKR compliance checks in about 60 seconds. It's self-serve with a free tier (no card required) and works whether or not your project runs on the JKR BINAXONE CDE. Install it at plugins.jkrbinaxone.com.
What happens to the model after handover?
It keeps working. The verified as-built model becomes the backbone of a BIM-based CMMS: a live asset register, planned and reactive maintenance, and space and lifecycle management, all run on the same model your project team built. Facilities teams inherit complete, structured asset data on day one instead of a box of PDFs.
How is JKR BINAXONE priced?
Three tiers (Starter, Professional, Enterprise), each quoted to scope. Licensing can be structured as annual subscription or pay-per-project to match government procurement processes and private-sector contracts. Contact sales for a tailored proposal.
